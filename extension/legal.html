Privacy Policy

Subtitle text and API keys do not pass through servers operated by this project. Translation requests go directly from your computer to your chosen translation provider. Saved subtitles and translations stay on your computer. You can keep your Google API key in a local file at a path you choose.

The Software is designed to keep your data on your own computer. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developers do not receive any of your data.

Data the Software uses

  • Subtitle text and timing of the Hulu or Disney+ video you are watching, read from data the Hulu or Disney+ player already loaded in your browser.
  • Your settings (on/off, target language, translator, subtitle size and position), stored in your browser's extension storage.
  • A pairing code that lets the extension talk to the local service on your computer.

The Software does not collect your name, email address, Hulu account, payment details, browsing history or viewing history.

Where the data goes

  • The extension sends subtitles only to the companion service running on your own computer (address 127.0.0.1), protected by the pairing code.
  • Local translator (Hy-MT2): subtitles never leave your computer.
  • Codex: subtitle text is sent to OpenAI through your own signed-in Codex account and uses its quota. See OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Google: subtitle text is sent to Google Cloud Translation with your own API key and billed to your Google Cloud account. See Google's privacy policy.

What is stored, and where

Completed translations (the source subtitles and their translation) are stored only on your computer, in the service's local database, so a title does not need to be translated twice. Translation progress of an unfinished title is kept only in memory. Logs stay on your computer. You can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the service's data and logs folders, and remove your settings by uninstalling the extension.
